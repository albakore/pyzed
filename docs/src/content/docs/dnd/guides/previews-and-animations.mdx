Everything you see during a drag is drawn natively, at the screen's frame rate, without
calling Python.

## The preview

The preview is what follows the pointer. By default it is a copy of the item, **lifted**: a bit
bigger (`lift=1.03`) with a soft `shadow`. It keeps the place where you grabbed it, so it
doesn't jump under the pointer.

```python
pz.draggable(card, "c1", lift=1.06, shadow="#00000080", opacity=0.9)
pz.draggable(card, "c1", lift=1.0, shadow=False)              # flat, like a design tool
pz.draggable(card, "c1", preview=ui.badge("1 file"))           # anything else
pz.draggable(dot, "out", preview="wire")                       # a cable (node editors)
pz.sortable(..., preview="none")                               # the items move, no preview
```

## The source

The original item, while its preview moves:

| `source=` | Effect | Used for |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `"ghost"` | Faded to `source_opacity` (0.35). | Draggables, by default. |
| `"hide"` | Hidden: the item itself seems to move. | Sortables, by default; canvases. |
| `"keep"` | As it was. | Palettes, wires. |

In a sortable the other items close the slot of the dragged one, so `source="ghost"` shows its faded
copy at the landing place instead (the same as `placeholder="ghost"`).

## The placeholder

Where the item will land: `placeholder="shadow"` (a tinted box), `"ghost"` (a translucent copy
at `ghost_opacity`) or `"none"`. Sortables show a shadow by default and droppables nothing.
`placeholder_style` changes the look of the shadow:

```python
pz.sortable(..., placeholder="shadow",
            placeholder_style={"background": "#98c37922", "border_color": "#98c379", "radius": 10})
```

## Highlights

Targets light up while a drag they take is over them (`over_style`) or anywhere
(`active_style`). See [droppable](/pyzed/dnd/basics/droppable/#how-it-looks).

## Animations

- **Making room**: in a sortable, the other items slide aside to open a gap, smoothly.
- **Reordering**: when the order of a sortable changes, from a drop or from your code
  (sorting by name, a new item), the items glide to their new places in `animation`
  milliseconds (200; `0` turns it off).
- **Dropping**: the preview flies to its landing place in `drop_animation` milliseconds (180),
  then the item appears there.
- **Cancelling**: <kbd>Esc</kbd>, or a drop outside any target, flies the preview back home.

<Aside type="tip" title="Reduced motion">
`drop_animation=0` and `animation=0` turn off the flights and the glides: useful for the
"reduce motion" setting of your app.
</Aside>
