# Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We, as members, contributors and maintainers, pledge to make participation in
the Pyzed community a harassment-free experience for everyone, regardless of
age, body size, visible or invisible disability, ethnicity, sex
characteristics, gender identity and expression, level of experience,
education, socio-economic status, nationality, personal appearance, race,
religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

We pledge to act and interact in ways that contribute to an open, welcoming,
diverse, inclusive and healthy community.

## Our standards

Examples of behavior that contributes to a positive environment:

- Being kind, patient and respectful of differing opinions and experiences
- Giving and gracefully accepting constructive feedback
- Taking responsibility for our mistakes and learning from them
- Focusing on what is best for the community, not just for ourselves

Examples of unacceptable behavior:

- Sexualized language or imagery, and unwelcome sexual attention or advances
- Trolling, insulting or derogatory comments, and personal or political attacks
- Public or private harassment
- Publishing others' private information without their explicit permission
- Any other conduct that could reasonably be considered inappropriate in a
  professional setting

## Scope

This Code of Conduct applies in all community spaces (issues, pull requests,
discussions and any other project channel) and when someone officially
represents the project in public spaces.

## Enforcement

Report unacceptable behavior privately to the maintainer,
[@albakore](https://github.com/albakore), through GitHub (for example with a
[private security advisory](https://github.com/albakore/pyzed/security/advisories/new)
if no other private channel is available). Every report is reviewed promptly
and fairly, and the privacy of the reporter is respected.

Maintainers may remove, edit or reject comments, commits, code, issues and
other contributions that do not follow this Code of Conduct, and may
temporarily or permanently ban anyone whose behavior is harmful, following
these guidelines:

1. **Correction**: a private, written warning explaining the problem.
2. **Warning**: a warning with consequences for continued behavior.
3. **Temporary ban** from interacting with the community.
4. **Permanent ban** for a pattern of violations or severe misconduct.
